## Question Type Breakdown

### Seating Arrangement (20 questions)
- **Types:** Circular arrangement, Linear arrangement, Direction-based
- **Sub-categories:**
  - Circular with n persons (fix one, deduce others)
  - Linear arrangement with constraints (corner positions)
  - Direction-based (clockwise/counterclockwise)
- **Strategy:** Practice 2-3 sets weekly, focus on fixing one position first

### Puzzles (20 questions)
- **Types:** Scheduling, Ranking, Matching
- **Sub-categories:**
  - Scheduling puzzle: n items in time slots (constraint satisfaction)
  - Ranking puzzle with conditions (elimination approach)
  - Matching puzzle with categories (cross-reference method)
- **Strategy:** Practice 2-3 sets weekly, focus on constraint elimination

### Syllogism (16 questions)
- **Types:** Statements with conclusions, Coded syllogism, Reverse syllogism
- **Approach:** Venn diagram approach for statements with conclusions
- **Symbol substitution** for coded syllogism
- **Reverse syllogism:** Conclusion to statement mapping
- **Strategy:** 1-2 sets weekly, Venn diagram method is fastest
